I fell in love with programming in 6th grade when I learned that my friend could develop the games I played on my PC. I adore reading, writing, thinking about, improving, and understanding programs.

I have experience working across diverse data modalities—including language, audio, video, images, graphs, and biological data—and have published research in machine learning, computer vision, software engineering, and AI systems.

I am also an active open-source contributor. My current projects include:

* **k3im** — Multi-backend 1D/2D/3D and spatiotemporal models for Keras 3
* **k3-node** — Multi-backend Graph Neural Networks for Keras 3
* **k3-addons** — Multi-backend extensions for Keras 3

My background spans software engineering, machine learning research, and open-source development, with experience across Keras, PyTorch, and JAX. I occasionally dabble in parallel programming frameworks such as CUDA. I also have a strong interest in Physical and Embodied AI.

Outside of research, I enjoy reading 📚 and strength training 💪.
