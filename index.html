🟢 Open to ML Systems & Research Roles

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science & Informatics at Oakland University, specializing in Machine Learning Systems, Agentic AI, and Deep Learning Reliability.

My work bridges foundational deep learning with high-performance systems engineering, spanning zero-dependency C++/ggml inference engines, multi-backend ML libraries, autonomous multi-agent code generation, and multimodal computer vision forensics.

I am an active open-source contributor to core Keras 3 (20+ merged PRs) and author of timm.cpp (C++ inference engine for 106 vision models), K3Node (multi-backend GNNs with 100% PyG parity), and Keras-Climate.

timm.cpp
C++17 / ggml

Standalone inference engine executing 106 PyTorch/timm architectures via GGUF with zero Python runtime dependency. Achieved up to 25.8× faster cold-start load times via zero-copy POSIX mmap and 3.4× to 5.8× calibrated quantization weight compression.
